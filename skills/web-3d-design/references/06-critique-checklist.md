# 06 — Web Design Critique Checklist

Run this on any site: your own builds, references, or Awwwards/FWA winners being studied. Score each section 1–5 and write one fix per low score.

## 1. First 5 seconds (above the fold)
- [ ] Do I know what this is and who it's for?
- [ ] Does the headline hook, and is it the dominant element?
- [ ] Does something move within the first second, with purpose?
- [ ] Is the next step obvious (scroll cue or CTA)?

## 2. Hierarchy & layout
- [ ] Squint test: does the reading order hold when blurred?
- [ ] Is there a consistent grid, with only intentional breaks?
- [ ] Is spacing on a scale, and do groups read as groups?
- [ ] Is it free of large unedited blocks of copy?
- [ ] LIFT: clear **leverage point**? Consistent **internal rhythm**? Is friction intentional and flow kept? Does it **transfer** (thumbnail, dark/light, mobile)? (see 10)
- [ ] Temporal flow: do sections alternate punch / linger / pulse / release, or is every section the same intensity?

## 3. Typography & colour
- [ ] Are there 2 typefaces at most, a clear scale, and comfortable body text?
- [ ] Does the type match the brand vibe (e.g. a script italic fighting a ribbon path = wrong)?
- [ ] Is there one accent colour, is the CTA the most saturated element, and does contrast pass AA?

## 4. Art direction & depth
- [ ] Is the vibe consistent with the brand keywords on every page?
- [ ] Does it have depth: light source, shadows, layering? Or does everything feel sandwiched flat between glass?
- [ ] Is focus selective rather than evenly blurry? Is there a vignette to pull the eye in?
- [ ] Are the details art-directed (props, textures, edges) and true to the subject?
- [ ] Is the imagery bespoke rather than generic stock? Is it high-res, sharp, well lit, cropped and graded to the brand? (see 12)
- [ ] Does the hero avoid a rotating carousel and commit to one focal message? (see 11)
- [ ] Is the boxiness reduced (blended edges, overlaps, organic shapes) where the brand allows?

## 5. Motion & 3D
- [ ] Does each animation have a job? Does motion overlap with no dead frames?
- [ ] Are easing and duration appropriate, not linear and not sluggish?
- [ ] Does the 3D add meaning, or is it decoration slowing the page?
- [ ] Is it smooth (60fps), and is there a reduced-motion fallback?

## 6. UX
- [ ] Can I find the work or product in 1–2 clicks? Are there filters where needed?
- [ ] Are there multiple paths to key pages? Is the CTA repeated at the end?
- [ ] Do hover-only details have mobile equivalents?
- [ ] Is social proof real and relevant?
- [ ] Is the primary CTA a solid, high-contrast accent button (not a ghost/outline button)?
- [ ] Conversion test for each section: is it **clear** (who, what next, why care), **scannable**, and **motivating**?
- [ ] Is it designed for the actual audience rather than the designer's or client's taste?

## 7. Technical
- [ ] Lighthouse 90+? LCP < 2.5s? No layout shift?
- [ ] Does it work by keyboard, with alt text and semantic headings?
- [ ] Does it hold up on a real phone and in Safari?
