# Low poly: background and sources

Low poly began as a technical necessity in real-time 3D (1990s games: Star Fox, Virtua Fighter, PS1/N64 titles) and became a deliberate aesthetic in the 2010s across indie games (Monument Valley-era stylised 3D, Firewatch-adjacent landscapes, A Short Hike), illustration, motion design and web backgrounds (Delaunay-triangulated "polygon" wallpapers). Core idea: visible facets with flat shading, consistent density, strong silhouettes, limited palettes, and light doing the work. 2D low poly converts images into triangle mosaics, typically with Delaunay triangulation.
