# Line art: background and sources

Line art reduces images to line: from Matisse's and Picasso's one-line drawings and Saul Steinberg's continuous-line cartoons to Hergé's *ligne claire*, technical drawing and blueprints, and today's monoline icons, editorial line illustration and "one-line" tattoos and branding. In motion, draw-on animation (pen reveals) became a staple via After Effects Trim Paths and SVG stroke-dash techniques; whiteboard animation and signature write-ons are popular uses.

Key guidance: monoline = one weight, clean and scalable; variable weight = thicker for weight/emphasis/nearness, thinner for detail; IBM's line style allows mixed weights only if clearly different, max four per illustration, on a 4 px grid; continuous line never lifts the pen and uses weight for depth; minimal line art relies on reduction and negative space.
