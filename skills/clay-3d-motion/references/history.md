# Clay 3D: background, studios and sources

Clay animation (claymation) is stop-motion with malleable plasticine figures, re-posed by hand between frames. Its look: soft forms, visible fingerprints, a slightly "boiling" surface, holds and choppy timing. Digital clay recreates this in 3D, either faithfully (stop-motion style) or as a smoothed, pastel "claymorphism" popular in brand, UI and social motion since about 2020.

| Reference | What to borrow |
|---|---|
| Aardman (Wallace & Gromit, Shaun the Sheep) | Expressive faces, fingerprints left in, charming handmade sets, comic timing. |
| Laika (Coraline, Kubo) | Replacement faces, rich miniature lighting and depth of field. |
| Will Vinton (coined "Claymation") | Morphing clay, fluid transformations. |
| Pingu, Morph | Simple plasticine characters, minimal sets, readable acting. |
| Claymorphism (UI/3D design trend) | Inflated soft 3D shapes, pastel colours, subtle inner shadows, friendly brand feel. |
| Clay (studio) | 3D brand and product motion for tech, polished soft-3D renders. |
| Remington Markham (bird claymation, Blender) | Animated fingerprint and Voronoi textures, hold frames, posterize in AE. |

Note: "clay render" in architecture/3D also means a plain grey untextured render for checking form and light. That's a different meaning; this skill is about the plasticine look.
