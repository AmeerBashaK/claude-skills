# Isometric: projection notes and sources

**True isometric** is an axonometric projection where all three axes are equally foreshortened (≈81.65%) and 120° apart; ground lines sit at 30° to the horizontal. A 3D camera gets it with an orthographic projection rotated 54.736° (= arctan √2) from vertical (35.264° down from horizontal) and 45° around the vertical axis. SSR construction: scale height 86.602% (cos 30°), shear ±30°, rotate ±30°.

**2:1 dimetric** ("pixel isometric") uses a 1:2 rise/run (≈26.565°) so lines stair-step cleanly on pixels; tiles are twice as wide as tall; in Blender it corresponds to a 60° X camera rotation. Strictly dimetric, though commonly called isometric. Games: Zaxxon (1982), Q*bert, SimCity 2000, Age of Empires, Monument Valley (true iso style), Hades.

**Lighting convention:** light from the front/top-left; the face toward the light brightest, top second (or top brightest in many illustration systems), the other side darkest; avoid lights directly behind or in front (flattens faces).

**Uses:** infographics, technical/architectural diagrams, product explainers, games, UI and brand illustration (e.g. IBM's isometric illustration style guidelines).
