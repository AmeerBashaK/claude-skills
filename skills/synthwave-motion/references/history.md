# Synthwave: background and sources

Synthwave (also outrun, retrowave) is a music genre and visual aesthetic that emerged in the late 2000s and 2010s from nostalgia for 1980s action films, video games, cartoons and synth soundtracks (Vangelis, John Carpenter, Tangerine Dream, Jan Hammer). The name "outrun" comes from Sega's 1986 arcade racer Out Run, known for its selectable soundtrack and 80s look. PC Gamer described the approach as taking elements of 80s excess and modernising them "so they're just barely recognizable". Key moments: Kavinsky and the *Drive* (2011) soundtrack, *Far Cry 3: Blood Dragon* (2013), *Kung Fury* (2015), *Hotline Miami*, and *Stranger Things* titles (2016).

| Reference | What to borrow |
|---|---|
| Out Run (1986), arcade racers | The sunset drive, palms, sports car, road to the horizon. |
| Tron (1982) | Laser grids, wireframe worlds, glowing lines on black. |

| Miami Vice | Pink/teal palette, neon signage, night drives. |
| Signalnoise (James White) | Definitive modern synthwave poster art: chrome, neon, gradients, symmetry. |
| Kung Fury, Far Cry 3: Blood Dragon | Over-the-top neon, VHS texture, laser excess. |
| Darksynth (Perturbator, Carpenter Brut) | Black/red cyberpunk menace. |
