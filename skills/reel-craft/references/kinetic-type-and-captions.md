# Kinetic type and captions

This file is the rulebook for text in motion in a reel: typeface classes, size pairing, how words arrive, get emphasised and leave, number behaviour, caption placement per aspect ratio, and talking-head and lyric edits. Every rule lists the batch-01 reel ids that show it ("one reel" means one). Frames are at 30 fps; 20, 24 and 25 fps reels are converted by time. Type fundamentals live in the typography skill, hierarchy in layout-composition. The reel key is at the end.

## Quick rules
1. Two or three type classes per reel [DZCY6bjOsUQ, DcL6By1z4QE, DeZ3P_8NeHT, DdwCXaQIHNU, Dd_t1ZhNAoR].
2. In loud or typographic reels type is on screen by frame 12 (eight reels: [DZCY6bjOsUQ, Dd_t1ZhNAoR, DcGaekAMU90, DcL6By1z4QE, DcYXIWbxzAN, DdCH-tFy7Wp, DclkKscsRbs, DdjM8xRNzk_]). Phone-shot reels start at frame 36 to 42 [DeaJ4yUg95f, DeXtJrAtOcr].
3. One word per 6 to 10 frames; hold the finished line 9 frames minimum, 15 for a seven-word headline.
4. One emphasis device on one word per beat; where a reel uses an emphasis colour, keep it to one [DZCY6bjOsUQ, Dd_t1ZhNAoR, DeXDu86snYE].

**Pairing**
1. One huge hero word, tiny connectors: heavy noun with small italic glue words [DcL6By1z4QE]; italic serif sub-line under heavy caps [DdwCXaQIHNU]; small body under a huge serif [DZCY6bjOsUQ].
2. Escalate size inside a line, each word larger than the last, over 0.6 to 1.8 s; the biggest may enter ghosted, 40 percent to solid in 6 frames [DZCY6bjOsUQ].
3. Accent colour on one word per line, often the last [DZCY6bjOsUQ, Dd_t1ZhNAoR, DeXDu86snYE, DePvWkxNAun].
4. In word-per-shot runs give each word its own ground colour [DdwCXaQIHNU].

## 4. Emphasis
One device, one word, per beat.
1. Accent colour, light pink to full red in 6 frames [DZCY6bjOsUQ]. Colour can carry meaning: red lost, magenta emphasis [Dd_t1ZhNAoR]; blue action, green success [DeY58dONeuY].
2. Size jump, ghost entry, or a weight or tracking bump [DZCY6bjOsUQ, DeRpl8Yhrb2, DcGaekAMU90, Dd6Uc27ssjp]; italic serif swap in mono captions [DeZ3P_8NeHT].
3. Underline draw, 10 to 12 frames (8 frames at 20 fps), or a circle drawn round the word [Dd_t1ZhNAoR]; underline snapping in from the left [DeF8z3XoLdh].
4. Highlighter or selection: marker fill [Dd_t1ZhNAoR]; yellow swipe [Dd6Uc27ssjp]; yellow per caption word every 6 frames [DeMrHF6qi0z]; cyan selection block [DclkKscsRbs]; bracket breathing 2 px [DeZOFtEMkuA].

## 5. Text as object
- A letter becomes the object: the O is a ball or portal the camera enters, then returns as tunnel, grid centre and sun [DdwCXaQIHNU, DeF8z3XoLdh].
- Type becomes data: a line spreads with horizontal blur, compresses to a waveform in 6 frames, swells; a waveform later sits between two words [DeaJ4yUg95f].
- Letters regroup into a waving hand, burst, reform as the next word [DcYXIWbxzAN]. Image as glyph [Dcs-MiOpO2V]. Letters built from blocks [Dc0izivoZNw]. Type to particles to a dot globe [DeRpl8Yhrb2].
- Depth: a two-way scrolling text wall, about 20 px per frame [DeF8z3XoLdh]; type behind the 3D object [Dc0izivoZNw, DdwCXaQIHNU].

## 6. Counters and number rolls
- Ease out: a stat counter's step sizes fall 170, 61, 13, 3, 1 across 30 frames; a ring gauge runs an S-curve, steps 1, 24, 59, 14, 1 [DdwCXaQIHNU]. Tiles count over 30 to 42 frames with vertical blur [DdY8V8XKJD6]; a digit roll lasts about 12 frames [Db28edFqHTP].
- Large number, small unit: three ticks in 0.8 s, then a separate figure slams in with a 3-frame flash [DZCY6bjOsUQ].
- Money escalates in three steps 0.7 s then 0.35 s apart (21 then 10 frames), each with a glow flash; a badge steps every 0.35 s (about 10 frames); a giant figure types digit by digit, grows, whites out [Dd_t1ZhNAoR].
- Countdown HUD: steps one unit per frame (211 minus the frame index, 211 to 1 across about 210 frames; an early note read it as 6 per 6 frames and was wrong), turns from white to red near the end [DeXDu86snYE].
- Sound (inferred from spectrograms): 2 to 8 kHz ticks mark typing and pop-ins in some reels [Dd_t1ZhNAoR, DeY58dONeuY, DXzurbATRQT, DclkKscsRbs]; typing, digit rolls and count-ups were silent in others [DeO1Rh7x5Qm, DePvWkxNAun, Db28edFqHTP, DdY8V8XKJD6].

## 7. Photographed screens, legibility, safe areas
1. Phone-shot-of-a-screen reels (5 of 6 have one) keep the fixed caption outside the screen. On-screen lines arrive one per beat, then blur out [DeaJ4yUg95f]; opening words as tiny caps 6 to 9 frames apart [DdjM8xRNzk_].
2. Speech-synced captions run 1 to 3 words [DeZ3P_8NeHT, Dd6Uc27ssjp]; long blocks only if they build at speech rate, ten lines over 7 s [DcL6By1z4QE].
3. Contrast: white on dark or gradient in most reels; red serif on cream [DZCY6bjOsUQ]; navy on pale violet [DcL6By1z4QE]; white with soft blue shadow on bright sky [DdCH-tFy7Wp]. On busy mixed media use a solid bar or pill (seen in one reel [DeMrHF6qi0z]).
4. Never cover a face: demo on top, speaker in the bottom third [DeZ3P_8NeHT].
5. Clip type at the frame edge only in transit, settling in 6 to 18 frames [DZCY6bjOsUQ, DcGaekAMU90].
6. Platform interface overlays were not measured: check current safe-area guidance first.

## 8. Talking-head dialogue captions
Mono or heavy sans, uppercase, letter-spaced, 1 to 3 words per unit, 0.3 to 1.0 s each, centred at about 50 to 70 percent of height (measured 49 to 57); emphasis words switch to italic serif or magenta; captions move to the top over illustrated plates [DeZ3P_8NeHT]. Cut on the first syllable of the next phrase, not the breath: 9 of 11 cuts within 80 ms of an audio onset against 53 percent chance, a modest result from one reel [DZCY6bjOsUQ]. The CTA keyword is bold accent italic serif in quotes, built over 60 frames while the presenter points at the comment field [DeZ3P_8NeHT].

## 9. Lyric-video type
- One type animation per lyric line, timed to the vocal: measured syllable grid 0.22 s (eighth notes, about 136 BPM) [DeRpl8Yhrb2]; observed reveals every 0.2 to 0.4 s [DeMrHF6qi0z].
- One highlighted word per line: colour, size or font swap; 3 to 4 words at a time with the last bold; glitch scatter on one word [DeRpl8Yhrb2].
- Type is the transition: a word dissolving into a curtain, or growing to fill the frame under heavy motion blur [DeRpl8Yhrb2].
- Bookend with the same typed terminal card at start and end; type section headings with a block caret to make acts [DeRpl8Yhrb2]. Hard cuts fall on lyric turns about every 3 s (3.0, 6.4, 9.0 s) [DeMrHF6qi0z]; its cut-to-beat sync measured below chance on only about three true cuts, so treat it as untested.

## 11. Build recipes (five most reused moves)
Reels showing each: word-by-word 15, one-word emphasis about 14, typed text 11, blur-in 7, count-up 7. GSAP assumes a paused seekable timeline, no random values. In After Effects use set_expression for expressions and run_extendscript for animators (ADBE Text Animator, ADBE Text Expressible Selector).

**1. Word-by-word.** Animator Opacity 0%, expression selector Based On Words, Amount:
```
var step=0.25, fade=0.1;  // 7.5 frames per word, 3-frame fade
var p=linear(time-inPoint,(textIndex-1)*step,(textIndex-1)*step+fade,0,1);
100*(1-p)
```
GSAP: `tl.from(".w",{opacity:0,y:8,duration:0.1,stagger:0.25,ease:"power2.out"},0.2)`. Remotion: `interpolate(frame-6-i*8,[0,3],[0,1],{extrapolateLeft:"clamp",extrapolateRight:"clamp"})` per word i.

**2. Typed with caret.** Source Text expression, left-aligned:
```
var s="...", cps=14;   // 14 to 32 in fields, 7.5 to 10 for headlines
var n=Math.floor(Math.max(0,time-0.2)*cps);
s.substr(0,n)+((Math.floor(time/0.5)%2)?"":"|")  // blink period untested
```
GSAP: tween `{n:0}` to `s.length` over `s.length/cps`, ease "none", write `s.slice(0,Math.round(o.n))`. Remotion: `s.slice(0,Math.floor(Math.max(0,frame-6)*cps/fps))`. A cursor arrives 9 to 15 frames before what it triggers [DeY58dONeuY].

**3. Blur-in and out.** Gaussian Blur high to 0, Opacity 0 to 100, a few px of Position Y, over 3 frames, ease out. GSAP: `fromTo(el,{opacity:0,filter:"blur(14px)",y:6},{opacity:1,filter:"blur(0px)",y:0,duration:0.1,ease:"power2.out"})`.

**4. One-word emphasis.** Animator on the word (Fill Color to accent, Scale up), Range Selector, Based On Words, Units Percentage, Start and End at 100*(i-1)/N and 100*i/N (N words, word i). Underline or highlighter: rectangle, Scale X 0 to 100% from the left over 12 frames, ease out. Font swap: cut between two stacked layers over 8 to 10 frames [DcGaekAMU90]. GSAP: `tl.to(word,{color:A,scale:1.12,duration:0.1}).fromTo(ul,{scaleX:0},{scaleX:1,transformOrigin:"0 50%",duration:0.4},"<")`.

**5. Count-up.** Slider named n keyframed 0 to N over 30 frames, ease out. Text expression:
```
Math.round(effect("n")("Slider")).toString().replace(/\B(?=(\d{3})+(?!\d))/g,",")
```
Add vertical Directional Blur, length `Math.abs(effect("n")("Slider").velocity)*k`; use tabular figures. GSAP: tween `{v:0}` to N, 1 s, "power3.out", write `Math.round(o.v)`. Remotion: `interpolate` with `Easing.out(Easing.cubic)` over 30 frames.

## 12. Limits
Timings were read from contact sheets at about 5 fps plus every frame around cuts, so treat them as plus or minus 2 frames; derived rates are computed from those, and the median word interval covers nine reels. Sound statements are inferred from spectrograms, not heard; key and tempo are estimates. Only 7 of 26 sheets of the 6-minute lyric video were viewed [DeRpl8Yhrb2]. The sample is 30 promo and showcase reels from one platform in one season (18 vertical, 9 landscape, 3 square), nearly all Latin script (one showreel shows another [DcOIGDdss_L]); there is no evidence for news, long-form subtitling or other scripts.
