# Format recipes

Beat-sheet recipes for the twelve reel formats in the 30-reel study. Pick a format, copy its beats into a shot list, take the look from the matching style skill and the sound from sound-design.md.

**Limits.** 30 reels, one short-form platform, one season; single-reel formats say so. Average shot = duration / (hard cuts + 1) from meta.md; the cut detector over-counts flashes. Lens figures are estimated, tempo and key inferred, failure modes inferred from what the reels avoid. Frames assume 30 fps (25 of 30 reels).

## 1. SaaS / UI promo with floating glass cards
**Use when:** software, screens, no footage.
**Duration:** 12.0-32.9 s, 0-4 hard cuts, a UI state change every 0.7-2 s [Db28edFqHTP, DdY8V8XKJD6, DeY58dONeuY, DXzurbATRQT, DclkKscsRbs, DeZOFtEMkuA, DeO1Rh7x5Qm].
**Beats:**
1. 0-1.5 s: cards drifting at different Z speeds on a mesh gradient, or a line and dot on black [Db28edFqHTP, DXzurbATRQT].
2. 1.4-2.0 s: logo letters assemble over the gradient [Db28edFqHTP]. Or hold the lockup for the last 1.1-2.7 s, assembled from blur [DdY8V8XKJD6] or flashed in [DXzurbATRQT].
3. 3-14 s: 6-10 states, each a pill growing into a card in 0.4-0.6 s, held 0.3 s or more; text enters after the previous element left [Db28edFqHTP]. Then proof tiles (format 12), then the lockup hold.
**Look:** one gradient ground as the constant; thin-rimmed glass cards; one accent whose hue carries meaning (blue action, green success); cursor arrives 0.3-0.5 s before its effect [DeY58dONeuY, DeZOFtEMkuA]. Skill: glassmorphism-motion.
**Motion:** pill-to-card morph, 3-4 frame cross-blur [DclkKscsRbs, DeO1Rh7x5Qm], 5-10 degree tilts that settle flat [DeY58dONeuY]. AE: Rectangle Path Size and Roundness; a masked adjustment layer with Gaussian Blur as backdrop.
**Sound:** bass-forward bed, ticks at contacts only, sparse sub hits (four in [DXzurbATRQT]), wipes and count-ups silent [Db28edFqHTP, DdY8V8XKJD6].
**Fails:** no holds; new text over old text; two accents.

## 2. Phone-shot of a screen, single take
**Use when:** only screen motion, live-demo credibility wanted.
**Duration:** 10.3-26.5 s, 0-1 hard cuts [Db28edFqHTP, DdY8V8XKJD6, DdjM8xRNzk_, DeSvnCQIgs3, DeXtJrAtOcr, DeaJ4yUg95f].
**Beats (about 22 s):**
1. 0-1.0 s: a hand leaves the keyboard (out at 0.4, 0.8, 1.0 s), screen dark or quiet [DdjM8xRNzk_, DeXtJrAtOcr, DeaJ4yUg95f].
2. 2.0 s: first full-form state lands with the audio drop [DeSvnCQIgs3].
3. 2.0 s to end minus 3 s: screen mood or content changes every 1.0-1.5 s by gradient wipe, flash fill or blur ramp, never a cut [DdY8V8XKJD6, DdjM8xRNzk_]; at most one 0.3-0.7 s rack focus [Db28edFqHTP].
4. Last 1-3 s: hero hold, camera drifting up 12-15 px [Db28edFqHTP, DdjM8xRNzk_].
**Look:** dark room, LED bar, a top caption that never moves [Db28edFqHTP, DdY8V8XKJD6, DeSvnCQIgs3]. The screen is the key light, so its mood recolours the room: maroon, orange, white, maroon [DdjM8xRNzk_], or a 9-10 s hue loop [DeaJ4yUg95f].
**Motion:** handheld, about 26 mm phone-lens look, slow push. AE fake: Corner Pin onto a screen plate.
**Sound:** one bed on a fixed grid; low end removed for 4 beats (6.46-8.53 s), returned on the biggest picture change [Db28edFqHTP].
**Fails:** any cut; a drifting caption.

## 3. Kinetic type or logo sting
**Use when:** title, intro or brand reveal; no footage.
**Duration:** 6.3-15.2 s, 2-16 hard cuts, average shot 0.4-2.1 s [DcYXIWbxzAN, Dcs-MiOpO2V, DdwCXaQIHNU, DeF8z3XoLdh].
**Beats (15 s) [DeF8z3XoLdh]:**
1. 0-1.6 s: a ball drops in at 0.4 s and becomes a letter, bounce and settle in 0.8 s.
2. 1.8-8.0 s: crash zoom into the ball, tunnel flight, then geometric scenes such as a rippling cube field.
3. 8.0-10.0 s: one verb per scene, 0.4-0.6 s each, each on its own colour field and animated as the verb it names: a 4x squeeze released with overshoot, a 360 spin [one word per colour also in DdwCXaQIHNU].
4. 10.2-15.2 s: swarm to helix, then the wordmark falls together, letters from -30 and +40 degrees to 0 in 0.4 s [DdwCXaQIHNU]; hold about 1 s.
**Look:** one reusable object (the O) as letter, portal, grid centre, sun [DeF8z3XoLdh, DdwCXaQIHNU]; glow = blurred stroke copy plus a white outline copy 1-2 frames behind [DcYXIWbxzAN]. Skills: swiss-style-motion, bauhaus-motion, y2k-chrome-motion.
**Motion:** hard cuts on the 0.25 or 0.5 s grid [DdwCXaQIHNU, DeF8z3XoLdh]: 15 frames, or 7.5 (alternate 7 and 8, or use a 60 fps comp).
**Sound:** track an exact number of beats (12 beats = 6.72 s at 107.1 BPM [Dcs-MiOpO2V]); 1-1.5 s decay on the final hold.
**Fails:** one background for all words; off-grid cuts; no lockup hold.

## 4. Glitch / hype edit
**Use when:** 10+ live shots and a hard track. One reel [DZUZITfz0Qu].
**Duration:** 14.7 s, 26 hard cuts (average shot 0.55 s), median frame change 13.4 against 0.7-2.7 in the calm reels.
**Beats:**
1. 0-2.9 s: clean wide shot with a 5-6 frame flash-in, then a strip glitch: 4-8 horizontal bands re-placed every 1-2 frames , 1 frame inverted, 1 normal.
2. 2.9-14.6 s: ten cuts, shots of 0.56-1.83 s, each bridged by one device: strip displacement, inversion flash, circle-to-X mask, blur snap-in (first frame blurred, sharp in 4-5), torn card spinning 4-8 degrees per frame, barcode bars, recolour strobe (3 recolours in 5 frames). End on the opening composition, then black.
**Look:** static low-angle footage, about 24-35 mm, subject centred; all movement added in post.
**Motion:** AE: Posterize Time at 15 fps, masked slices with Offset, Invert toggled by `Math.floor(time*30)%2`, Directional Blur on the first post-cut frame. Keep 2 clean frames between bursts.
**Sound:** gated tone about 90 ms apart, falling sweep onto the cut, 0.33 s mute then a hit 60 ms before the cut.
**Fails:** camera moves instead of post; no clean reset frame; hits after the cut.

## 5. Tutorial or explainer, talking head plus screen recordings
**Use when:** teaching, with a speaker or voice-over and screen proof.
**Duration:** 35.1-95.5 s, 12-41 hard cuts, average shot 1.6-4.9 s [DeZ3P_8NeHT, DcGaekAMU90, Dd6Uc27ssjp, Dd_t1ZhNAoR].
**Beats (63 s) [DeZ3P_8NeHT]:**
1. 0-12.3 s: illustrated hook built by code.
2. 12.7-22 s: speaker, locked medium close-up, about 85 mm shallow-depth look.
3. 22.3-24.7 s: screen recording on the top two thirds, speaker in the bottom third. 27-56 s: screen recordings and illustrated plates only; a caption phrase every 0.5-1 s throughout.
4. 56.7-63 s: speaker returns, guide mock-up slides up, call to action (format 10).
**Look:** three worlds alternate, illustrated, speaker, screen; or three background moods as chapters [Dd6Uc27ssjp]. Captions narrate: 1-3 words, 0.2-0.7 s per word, lower third [DeZ3P_8NeHT] or mid-frame [Dd6Uc27ssjp, DcGaekAMU90]; emphasis in italic serif or accent colour [DeZ3P_8NeHT, DcGaekAMU90]. UI chrome (selection boxes, cursor) is the transition language [DcGaekAMU90]. Skills: typography, explainer-documentary.
**Sound:** dry mono voice, LRA 2-4 [DeZ3P_8NeHT, DcGaekAMU90, Dd6Uc27ssjp]; bed removed or automated, never sidechain-ducked (none seen) [DeXDu86snYE, Dd_t1ZhNAoR, DcGaekAMU90]; cut on a phrase's first syllable (seen in one reel, 9 of 11 cuts) [DZCY6bjOsUQ].
**Fails:** cutting on the breath. The speaker is absent 27-56 s here, so keeping a face on screen is a choice, not a proven rule.

## 6. Showreel
**Use when:** 20+ finished pieces. One reel [DcOIGDdss_L].
**Duration:** 84.1 s, 62 hard cuts, average shot 1.3 s.
**Beats:**
1. 0-10 s: collage-desk intro, name chips, reel title.
2. 10-55 s: 25-30 pieces of 30-90 frames, each looping one complete motion; title cards recur every 20-30 s in one type.
3. 55-80 s: lower-third pill chips label pipeline stages (the only repeating UI).
4. 80.7-84.1 s: badge logo, 3.4 s hold.
**Build:** one comp per piece of 30-90 frames that loops one complete motion, then a master comp that butts them with cuts.
**Look:** a new style per piece; cuts on matching colour or direction, whips. Skill: motion-reel.
**Sound:** 120 BPM, wide dynamics by design: 1.4 s dropout, chopped section 38-56 s on the 0.5 s grid, 3.4 s silence under the badge.
**Fails:** no repeated device; pieces over 3 s.

## 7. Lyric video or long form with chapters
**Use when:** a finished song over 2 minutes.
**Duration:** 377.9 s, 17 hard cuts in meta.md (a frame-difference detector finds about 72 shots) [DeRpl8Yhrb2]; a 12 s snippet [DeMrHF6qi0z]; numbered chapter cards [DcGaekAMU90].
**Beats [DeRpl8Yhrb2]:**
1. 0-2.7 s: terminal-style title card, typed caption, block cursor.
2. Bass out is verse picture, bass in is chorus picture; change picture on bass entries (9.0, 49.97, 278.9 s), not every beat.
3. 239-254 s: quiet valley, so 360-370 s reads as the peak. 275.4-278.9 s: silence, then 14 cuts in 3.9 s.
4. 376-377.8 s: the card returns, portrait now a line drawing.
**Build:** one comp, one continuous camera; a per-line text animator timed to the vocal; particle or mosaic dissolves between scenes.
**Look:** kinetic type in one world, each line timed to the vocal with one highlighted word (colour, size or font swap); particle dissolves as transitions; one dot-grid texture on everything; virtual camera only. Snippet: picture changes every 3 s on lyric turns [DeMrHF6qi0z]. Skills: particle-motion, pixel-art-motion.
**Sound:** finished song as the only bed, vocal level flat.
**Fails:** cutting every beat (about nine hand-placed matches in 71 cuts); no bookend.

## 8. Hero-object macro ad
**Use when:** one object carries the brand. One reel [DeXDu86snYE].
**Duration:** 29.7 s, 3 hard cuts, pop at 23.0 s (77%).
**Beats:**
1. 0-7.2 s: object at 60% of frame, lens label, frame countdown stepping one unit per frame from 211 (211 minus the frame index), slow push; needle touches (spark 3.4-3.8 s), crater opens, countdown ends at 001.
2. 7.2-16.0 s: held "almost" frame, HUD fades, captions at 0.25 s per word; a 0.4 s scan line turns the render to wireframe (11.6-12.0 s) and back.
3. 16.0-21.8 s: black, then a phone-feed mock-up with rapid image swaps: the metaphor becomes the pitch.
4. 21.8-25.0 s: cut back, pop at 23.0 s, shreds, star, wordmark. 26.0-29.7 s: pill call to action, hold.
**Look:** near-black stage, one saturated hue, rim light, specular streak, HUD of REC dot, timecode, lens label. Skill: cinematography-for-ai.
**Motion:** AE Source Text countdown: `n=Math.max(1,211-timeToFrames(time)); ("000"+n).slice(-3)`, one step per frame, red from about 043.
**Sound:** drone and riser 0-7 s, hard cut-out 7.0-7.4 s, voice alone, sub impact 21.5-22.0 s as the crater lights, noise crack at the pop (23.0 s), 0.2 s near-silence (24.03-24.23 s) before the wordmark.
**Fails:** popping early; static HUD labels; two hues.

## 9. Icon or shape morph chain
**Use when:** icons, logo reveals.
**Duration:** 6.3-103.8 s, 0-4 hard cuts [Dbmcud4IexE, DXzurbATRQT, DeSvnCQIgs3, DcYXIWbxzAN].
**Beats (15-30 s):**
1. 0.2 s: a dot or line appears [DXzurbATRQT].
2. 8-20 states, each held 0.7-1.3 s, one rest of about 1.7 s; a white flash resets the scene [Dbmcud4IexE, DXzurbATRQT].
3. Collapse into the wordmark, hold 1 s [DeSvnCQIgs3].
**Look:** muted ground, one saturated accent per icon [Dbmcud4IexE]. Skills: flat-2d-motion, line-art-motion.
**Motion:** each morph changes one property (shape, then colour, then scale) over 0.3-0.5 s (9-15 frames), ease-out and settle; the link is a geometric echo, never a dissolve; rotation bridges a change in object count [Dbmcud4IexE]. AE: Path keyframes between equal-vertex paths, Trim Paths draw-ons.
**Sound:** one tonal bed, accents on the biggest jumps only, 1.5 s fade [Dbmcud4IexE].
**Fails:** dissolves; shape, colour and scale at once.

## 10. Comment-bait or call-to-action template
**Use when:** the goal is comments or bookings.
**Duration:** 8.1-12.4 s, 0-1 hard cuts, all three 1:1 reels in the set [DdCH-tFy7Wp, DclkKscsRbs, DeZOFtEMkuA]. Longer reels close with a 2-4 s version [DeZ3P_8NeHT, DeXDu86snYE, DePvWkxNAun].
**Beats (8 s) [DdCH-tFy7Wp]:**
1. 0-0.6 s: a blob blooms with a big number.
2. 0.4-2.0 s: offer title, file icons fan out with a ring pulse; fade to white.
3. 2.8-5.4 s: a search pill types the keyword, a hand cursor enters at 3.6 s, clicks, taps send.
4. 5.4-8.1 s: sign-off line types, avatar pops.
Pitch variant (12 s): dark hook, white UI, tapped card changes state, end card [DclkKscsRbs, DeZOFtEMkuA].
**Look:** three soft scene types (sky, white UI, signature); blur in and out about 4 frames [DclkKscsRbs]; the cursor times every beat. Skill: glassmorphism-motion.
**Sound:** 8-beat 3.04 s loop with the scene change on the loop boundary, hard stop on a beat at 8.04 s.
**Fails:** asking before showing the offer.

## 12. Dashboard or stat-tile count-up
**Use when:** proof of results. A 1.2-1.6 s beat inside a 15-23 s reel, never a whole reel [DdY8V8XKJD6, DdwCXaQIHNU, DeO1Rh7x5Qm, Db28edFqHTP].
**Beats (1.6 s):**
1. 0-0.2 s: cards slide in from different directions, 2-frame stagger, each fading from blur in 3 frames, settling with a bounce [DdwCXaQIHNU, DeO1Rh7x5Qm].
2. 0.2-1.2 s: numbers roll up over about 30 frames with vertical blur, last steps small (+170, +231, +244, +247, +248); ring gauges and bars grow with them [DdwCXaQIHNU, DdY8V8XKJD6].
3. 1.2-1.6 s: hold 0.4-0.5 s so values read [DdY8V8XKJD6].
**Look:** navy or white rounded cards. Elsewhere counters escalate in three steps with a glow flash [Dd_t1ZhNAoR].
**Motion:** AE: `Math.round(ease(time,t0,t1,0,248))` in Source Text plus Directional Blur. GSAP: `gsap.to(o,{v:248,duration:1,ease:"power2.out",onUpdate})`. Remotion: `interpolate(frame,[0,30],[0,248],{easing:Easing.out(Easing.cubic),extrapolateRight:"clamp"})`.
**Sound:** 3-5 mid ticks, last heavier [Dd_t1ZhNAoR], or silent [Db28edFqHTP, DdY8V8XKJD6].
**Fails:** linear count; no hold.

## Pick a format
| Goal | Assets | Format |
|---|---|---|
| Software demo | Screens (plus monitor, phone) | 2, or 1 + 12 |
| Brand or title reveal | Logo, one typeface | 3 |
| Energy from footage | 10+ shots, a track | 4 |
| Teach a method | Speaker, screen recordings | 5 |
| Win clients | 20+ finished pieces | 6 |
| Song or long story | Track, illustrations | 7 |
| Sell through one object | CG hero object | 8 |
| Showcase icons | Vector icons | 9 |
| Comments or bookings | One offer, a keyword | 10 |
| Argue with stock | Cutouts, voice-over | 11 |
| Prove results | Numbers, stat tiles | 12, inside another format |

## Pre-flight checklist
1. Canvas: 9:16 in 18 of 30 reels, 16:9 in 9, 1:1 in 3 (all call-to-action).
2. First motion within 0.4 s [DcYXIWbxzAN, DdwCXaQIHNU, DdCH-tFy7Wp, DeF8z3XoLdh], or a quiet head under 1.4 s while a hand leaves [DeXtJrAtOcr].
3. Every state holds 0.3 s or more; each move starts before the last ends (user rule).
4. One accent hue; one constant (ground, caption or HUD) [Db28edFqHTP, DZCY6bjOsUQ, DdwCXaQIHNU, DcGaekAMU90].
5. One text idea per beat; words build at 0.2-0.35 s each [DZCY6bjOsUQ, DcGaekAMU90, Dd6Uc27ssjp].
6. First big audio arrival near 2.0 s, or a hit on frame 1 [DdwCXaQIHNU, DeF8z3XoLdh, DeSvnCQIgs3, DcYXIWbxzAN]; score only the biggest events.
7. At 120 BPM cuts sit on 15-frame beats [DdwCXaQIHNU, DeF8z3XoLdh]; impacts land 0-60 ms before the cut [DZUZITfz0Qu, Db28edFqHTP]; swells may pre-lap 0.1-1 s [DXzurbATRQT].
8. End hold of 1.0-2.7 s, median 1.5 s (longer 3-5.7 s only for a quiet hold or hero spin) [DXzurbATRQT, DeO1Rh7x5Qm, DdY8V8XKJD6, DeF8z3XoLdh], then choose the audio tail.
9. Export at -14 LUFS, true peak -1 dBTP or lower (12 of 30 originals exceeded 0), mono check.
